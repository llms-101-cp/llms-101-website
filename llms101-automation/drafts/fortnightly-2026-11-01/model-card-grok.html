Grok is xAI's family of reasoning models. As of late 2026 the current flagship is Grok 4.7, released September 21, 2026, which xAI calls its best model to date. It has a 500,000-token context window, four reasoning levels, and costs $2/$6 per million tokens. Earlier versions such as Grok 4.5 (July 2026), Grok 4.3 (April 2026), and the Grok 4.20 variants (March 2026) are now older models, not the current standouts; note that Grok 4.3 and 4.20 held 1M-token context windows while Grok 4.7, 4.6, and 4.5 hold 500K. Grok 4.7 is the current flagship for code, agentic work, and general knowledge tasks, and a sensible default for most everyday requests.
