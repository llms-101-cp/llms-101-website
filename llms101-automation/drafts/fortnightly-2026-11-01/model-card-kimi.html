Kimi K3.1 Pro is Moonshot AI's current flagship, a post-trained successor to Kimi K3 launched on October 23, 2026 with a 1-million-token context window. It is a roughly 2.8-trillion-parameter model with improved coding and financial-analysis performance and lower API pricing — about $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, alongside a faster K3.1 Pro Highspeed variant. It builds on the earlier K3 line (including K3 Max and K3 Swarm Max) and the K2 family, and is designed for long-horizon coding, agentic tool use, and knowledge work across very large documents.
