Mistral AI's current lineup is led by Mistral Large 4 (version 26.10), the new frontier flagship released in public preview on October 6, 2026, a mixture-of-experts model with roughly 1.05 trillion total parameters (about 52 billion active) and a 1-million-token context window. Mistral published its open weights on Hugging Face on October 29, 2026, under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License, making it by far the largest model in the family and succeeding the 675B-parameter Mistral Large 3. The lineup also includes the balanced Mistral Medium 3.5 (released April 2026) and the budget-friendly, unified Mistral Small 4, making Mistral a strong pick for anyone who wants capable models with the option to self-host.
