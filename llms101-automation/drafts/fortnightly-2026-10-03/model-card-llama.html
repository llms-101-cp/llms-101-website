Llama is Meta's family of open-weight language models, including Llama 4 (Scout and Maverick) and the widely-deployed text-only Llama 3.3 70B, all available for free download and self-hosting. As of October 2026, Meta's frontier development has moved to its newer Muse line — the proprietary flagship Muse Spark 1.3 (released September 2, 2026, with a roughly 1-million-token context window) and the open-weight, Apache 2.0-licensed Muse Glimmer (a ~30-billion-parameter model released August 10, 2026 that runs on a single consumer GPU), reflecting a renewed open-weight commitment rather than a move away from it. Existing Llama models remain open and available, making them a practical choice for local chatbots, document summarization, and privacy-sensitive deployments.
