Gemini is Google DeepMind's flagship AI model family, currently led by Gemini 3.1 Pro (the released flagship, since Gemini 3.5 Pro remains in partner testing and is not yet public). Gemini 3.6 Flash became Google's new default workhorse model on July 21, 2026, succeeding Gemini 3.5 Flash with a 1 million-token context window, a March 2026 knowledge cutoff, and lower per-token pricing. It keeps the 1M-token context window and uses roughly 17 percent fewer output tokens than its predecessor because it takes fewer reasoning steps and tool calls to complete multi-step workflows. For high-volume, budget work, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite ($0.30 / $2.50 per million tokens) targets high-throughput, low-latency tasks like agentic search, document processing, and subagent fan-out.
