Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of multimodal models, led as of October 2026 by Gemini 3.8 Flash (released September 2, 2026), the current Flash workhorse that superseded the earlier 3.5, 3.6, and 3.7 Flash models. The lineup also includes Gemini 3.1 Pro as the higher-intelligence flagship (still in preview) and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite for ultra-cheap, high-volume tasks. Gemini 3.8 Flash handles text, image, audio, and video input across a 1-million-token context window and scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at high reasoning, making it a strong choice for long-document analysis, coding, and agent workflows.
