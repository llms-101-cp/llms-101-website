Grok is xAI's family of large language models, with Grok 4.7 serving as the current flagship since its September 21, 2026 release — it supersedes Grok 4.6 (August 12, 2026) and Grok 4.5 (July 8, 2026), all of which carry a 500,000-token context window. Grok 4.7 lists at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, and is tuned for coding, agentic tasks, and professional knowledge work. The broader lineup includes Grok 4.3 and Grok 4.20 with 1-million-token windows for long-document and high-throughput work, plus the coding-focused Grok Build 0.1; the older Grok 4.1 Fast was retired from the API on May 15, 2026.
