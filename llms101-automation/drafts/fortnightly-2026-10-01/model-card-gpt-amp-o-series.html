OpenAI's current flagship is GPT-6 Astra, which launched September 3, 2026, at $10/$50 per million tokens with a 1M-token context and gated cyber capabilities. It has a 1,050,000-token context window, a 128,000-token maximum output, and a knowledge cutoff of April 30, 2026. The family was rounded out when OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on 22 September 2026 as mid-tier and low-cost models below GPT-6 Astra, with Sol at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 — and on September 29 OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Sol as an upgrade to GPT-6 Sol at the same $2 input, $0.10 cached input, and $10 output rates per million tokens. The lineup suits long-context document work, agentic coding, and demanding professional reasoning, with cheaper variants for high-volume everyday tasks.
