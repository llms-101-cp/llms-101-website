Llama is Meta AI's open-weight model family, with the Llama 4 generation (Maverick and Scout) and the text-only Llama 3.3 70B remaining widely deployed options you can download and self-host for chat, writing, and coding. Note that Llama 4 is the last Llama-branded generation and no longer Meta's frontier model: Muse Spark 1.3, released September 2, 2026, is Meta's frontier proprietary reasoning model, delivered through Muse Code and the Meta Model API, with open weights named as a future roadmap item. On the open side, on August 10, 2026, Meta Superintelligence Labs shipped Muse Glimmer: a 30-billion-parameter multimodal model, Apache 2.0 licensed, with weights on Hugging Face, capable of running fully offline on a single consumer GPU — so Meta now runs a mixed open/closed strategy rather than the fully open posture Llama once represented.
