Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models. As of late 2026, the public lineup is Fable 5.1 (the most capable model, built for the hardest reasoning and long-horizon agentic work), Opus 5.5 (Anthropic's recommended default for most workloads, approaching Fable's performance at a lower price), Sonnet 5.5 (the best balance of speed and intelligence for everyday coding and writing), and Haiku 5.5 (the fastest, cheapest tier at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per million tokens on prompts up to 100K, released October 7, 2026). All four share a 1M-token context window and are well suited to writing, research, coding, and document analysis. A restricted-access model, Mythos 5.1, is also available through verification programs only.
