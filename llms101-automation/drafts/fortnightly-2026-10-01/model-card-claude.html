Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models, led as of October 2026 by the Mythos-class Fable 5.1 and the newest Claude 5.5 models, which cover the September 2026 launch of Claude Sonnet 5.5 plus releases such as Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Mythos 5.1, and Claude Sonnet 5 . The most recent release, Claude Sonnet 5.5, came out on September 28, 2026, as the second model in the Claude 5.5 family , and costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, the same as Sonnet 5, while running more than 30% faster . Sonnet 5.5 offers a 1M-token context window with up to 128K output tokens , making the lineup well suited to long-document analysis, research, and everyday writing and reasoning tasks; the fast, low-cost Haiku 4.5 rounds out the family for lighter work.
