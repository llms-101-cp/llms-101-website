Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models. As of late 2026, the public lineup is Fable 5.1 (the most capable model, built for the hardest reasoning and long-horizon agentic work), Opus 5.5 (Anthropic's recommended default for most workloads, approaching Fable's performance at a lower price), Sonnet 5.5 (the best balance of speed and intelligence for everyday coding and writing), and Haiku 5.5 (the fastest, cheapest tier at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per million tokens on prompts up to 100K, released October 7, 2026). All four share a 1M-token context window and are well suited to writing, research, coding, and document analysis. A restricted-access model, Mythos 5.1, is also available through verification programs only.

Grok is xAI's family of reasoning models. As of late 2026 the current flagship is Grok 4.7, released September 21, 2026, which xAI calls its best model to date. Meta AI Highly Competitive

Llama 4 Scout (17B active / 109B total), Llama 4 Maverick (17B active / ~400B total), Llama 3.3 70B, Llama 3.1 (8B, 70B, 405B)
Core superpower

As of November 2026, Meta AI's current open-weight Llama generation is Llama 4, released on April 5, 2025, with Scout (17B active / 109B total parameters and a 10 million token context window) and Maverick (17B active / 400B total and a 1 million token context window). Both use a Mixture-of-Experts architecture, and Scout's 10M context window is the largest of any publicly released open-weight model at its size class. The earlier Llama 3.x models (including Llama 3.3 70B and the Llama 3.1 family) are still downloadable but have been superseded. Separately, Meta's frontier flagship work now runs through its Muse line, which is no longer closed-only: Muse Spark launched proprietary in April 2026, but Meta released the open-weight Muse Glimmer (30B) on August 10, 2026, and Muse Spark Open (816B total / 42B active) on October 15, 2026. Llama remains a top choice for anyone who wants capable, freely downloadable models for long-document work.
