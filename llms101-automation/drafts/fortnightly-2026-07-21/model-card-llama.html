Muse Spark is Meta AI's current frontier model, built by Meta Superintelligence Labs and first launched April 8, 2026 as the company's first closed, proprietary model — a strategic shift away from its earlier open-weight Llama family. The upgraded Muse Spark 1.1 shipped July 9, 2026 with a 1-million-token context window and a paid, OpenAI-compatible Meta Model API priced at $1.25 per million input tokens and $4.25 per million output tokens, with $20 in free credits for new accounts. It's a multimodal reasoning model tuned for agentic tasks — tool use, computer use, and coding — and powers Meta AI across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, while the older Llama models remain the option for anyone who needs open weights to run or fine-tune locally.
