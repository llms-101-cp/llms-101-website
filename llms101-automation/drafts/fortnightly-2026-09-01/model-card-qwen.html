Qwen3.8-Max is Alibaba's flagship large language model, previewed on July 19, 2026 at the World AI Conference and officially released on August 3, 2026 through Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio. It is a 2.4-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 95 billion active parameters, a 1 million token context window, and native text, image and video input, priced at $2.00 per million input tokens and $6.00 per million output tokens. Open weights followed shortly after: the Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B checkpoint was published on Hugging Face on August 12, 2026 under a custom Qwen3.8-Max License (not Apache or MIT), though that checkpoint is text only and always reasons, making the hosted API the version to use for multimodal and full long-context work.
