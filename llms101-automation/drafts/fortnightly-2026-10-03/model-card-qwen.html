Qwen is Alibaba's family of large language models, led by the flagship Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with a 1-million-token context window that became generally available on August 3, 2026, alongside the earlier Qwen3.7-Max and Qwen3.7-Plus. The current open-weights entry is Qwen3.8-27B, a dense 27B multimodal model released August 14, 2026 under the permissive Apache 2.0 license, with 262K native context extensible to 1M; it supersedes the older Qwen3.6-27B as the default local Qwen and runs on a single GPU. The lineup is well suited to multilingual writing, long-document analysis, and coding, with the open 27B giving non-technical and technical users alike a free, self-hostable option.
