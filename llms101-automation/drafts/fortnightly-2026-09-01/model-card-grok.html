Grok, made by xAI, is led by Grok 4.6, the current flagship released on August 12, 2026 as a post-training upgrade built for long-running agents, coding, and visual work. It keeps the same roughly 1.5-trillion-parameter V9 foundation, 500K-token context window, and $2/$6 per million token pricing as the prior flagship, Grok 4.5 (released July 8, 2026), while adding improved reasoning; the lineup also includes the longer-context Grok 4.3 and the budget-friendly Grok 4.1 Fast. xAI has signaled a larger ~2.1-trillion-parameter Grok 4.7 and a Grok 5 for later in 2026, but neither has shipped yet, so Grok 4.6 remains the model to use today for demanding research, analysis, and app-building tasks.
