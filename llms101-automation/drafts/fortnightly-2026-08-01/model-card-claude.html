Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models, currently led at the Opus tier by Claude Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), which launched July 24, 2026 and replaced the previous-generation Opus 4.8 as the flagship at the same $5/$25 per-million-token pricing. The lineup also includes the top-end Fable 5, the high-value Sonnet 5 workhorse, and the lightweight Haiku 4.5; Opus 5, Sonnet 5, and Fable 5 each support a 1-million-token context window, letting you feed in book-length documents at once. Claude is a strong choice for long-context reading, summarizing, drafting, and careful analysis where accuracy and instruction-following matter more than rock-bottom cost.
