Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of multimodal AI models, spanning the higher-reasoning Pro tier and the efficiency-focused Flash tier. Both Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite feature a one-million-token input context window and a maximum output limit of 64,000 tokens. The lightweight Flash-Lite variant targets high-volume work: it is described as the fastest, most cost-effective 3.5-class model, delivering 350 output tokens per second according to the Artificial Analysis Index — a speed figure that belongs to Flash-Lite specifically, not to the standard Flash model, and is framed as fastest within its 3.5 series and price tier rather than fastest of every frontier model.
