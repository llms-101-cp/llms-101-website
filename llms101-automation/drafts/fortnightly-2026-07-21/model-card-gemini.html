Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of multimodal AI models, spanning the fast, efficient Gemini 3.5 Flash, the higher-intelligence Gemini 3.1 Pro, and the ultra-cheap Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite. Gemini 3.5 Flash offers a 1 million–token context window and is optimized for high-throughput coding and agentic ("tool-using") workflows, scoring 55 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index while running faster than most models in its intelligence tier. It is a strong pick for high-volume, multi-step tasks like document processing, research assistants, and automated workflows where speed and cost matter as much as raw capability.
