Grok is xAI's family of reasoning models, with Grok 4.3 and the Grok 4.20 variants (reasoning, non-reasoning, and multi-agent) as the current standouts. A common mix-up is the context window: Grok 4.3 has a 1 million-token context window, while the entire Grok 4.20 family runs at 2 million tokens — not 1 million — per xAI documentation and OpenRouter listings. That makes Grok 4.20 a strong pick for long-document analysis, large codebases, and multi-step agentic tasks that need the biggest possible working memory.
