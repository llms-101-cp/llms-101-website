Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models, spanning the lightweight Haiku, the balanced Sonnet, and the high-capability Opus and Fable tiers. Anthropic's newest and most capable model, Fable 5, was briefly suspended in June 2026 under a US export-control directive, but the US Commerce Department lifted those controls on June 30, 2026, and Anthropic restored Fable 5 globally starting July 1, 2026; as of July 22, 2026, it is available across Claude.ai, the Claude Platform, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. The Claude models support a 200K-token context window (roughly a long book's worth of text at once) and are widely used for reasoning, writing, and coding tasks.
