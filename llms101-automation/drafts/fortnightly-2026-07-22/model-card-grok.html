Grok is xAI's model family, now led by Grok 4.5, which launched July 8, 2026 for developers and July 9 for the public as the company's new flagship. It ships a 500K-token context window at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. Earlier releases including Grok 4.3, Grok 4.1, and Grok 4 Fast remain available, but independent testing by Artificial Analysis ranks Grok 4.5 near the frontier at a fraction of rivals' cost per task, making it the family's pick for coding, agent workflows, and knowledge work.
