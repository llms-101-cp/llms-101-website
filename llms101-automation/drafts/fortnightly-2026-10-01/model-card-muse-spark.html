Muse Spark is the frontier model family from Meta Superintelligence Labs, first introduced in April 2026 and now on its fourth release. Muse Spark 1.3, released September 2, 2026, is the current version and succeeds Muse Spark 1.2 (August 5, 2026) and Muse Spark 1.1 (July 9, 2026). It's a closed-weight, natively multimodal model with a roughly 1-million-token context window, aimed primarily at agentic and coding work, and it is rolling out across Meta's consumer surfaces including the Meta AI app, Instagram, and Facebook.
