Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of multimodal large language models, with the current generally available lineup built around Gemini 3.8 Flash (the fast, efficient workhorse released September 2, 2026), the more capable Gemini 3.1 Pro, and the ultra-cheap Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite for high-volume tasks. Gemini 3.8 Flash scores 59 on the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at high reasoning — up three points from Gemini 3.7 Flash — and carries a roughly 1-million-token context window. That combination makes Gemini well suited to long-document analysis, coding, and agentic workflows where you need strong reasoning without a premium price tag.
