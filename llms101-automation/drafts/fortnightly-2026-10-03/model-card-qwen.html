Qwen is Alibaba's family of large language models, led by Qwen3.8-Max, which launched on August 3, 2026 as a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total parameters, roughly 95 billion active per token, and a context window of up to one million tokens. Alibaba released the open weights as Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B in mid-August 2026 under a custom Qwen3.8-Max License, and the hosted model is available through providers such as OpenRouter and Together AI at roughly $2 per million input and $6 per million output tokens. The lineup also includes earlier releases like Qwen3.7-Max and Qwen3.7-Plus, with Qwen aimed primarily at coding, long-horizon agentic tasks, and research.
