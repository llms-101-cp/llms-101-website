Grok is xAI's chatbot family, available through X Premium subscriptions. Grok-2 and Grok-3 offer up to 128K context windows and integrate directly with X/Twitter's real-time data — a unique capability among major chatbots. While marketed for its less restrictive responses, independent benchmarks show it trails GPT-4 and Claude 3.5 on complex reasoning tasks, making it best suited for users who specifically need live social media intelligence.
