As of November 1, 2026, DeepSeek's current fast-tier model is DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, the October 17, 2026 refresh of the V4.1-Flash model that first launched September 10, 2026. It is served through the DeepSeek API under the same "deepseek-flash" model ID at unchanged pricing, and DeepSeek also released it as open weights on Hugging Face. The 1017 update reports notably higher scores than the original September checkpoint — for example, Humanity's Last Exam (without tools) rising from 36.8 to 42.6 and Terminal-Bench 4.0 from 31.2 to 42.3 — alongside shorter, faster outputs. With a 1-million-token context window, it's well suited to high-volume chat, document analysis, and coding or agentic workflows where latency and per-token cost matter most.
