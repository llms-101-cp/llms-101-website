Claude is Anthropic's family of AI models. As of October 2026, the current lineup includes Fable 5.1, the flagship Opus 5.5 (released September 22, 2026) and Sonnet 5.5 (released September 28, 2026), alongside the still-active Sonnet 5 and Haiku 4.5, plus the restricted-access Mythos 5.1. Opus 5.5 carries a 1M-token context window at $4 per million input and $20 per million output tokens, while Sonnet 5.5 runs the same 1M context at $2 input / $10 output, making it the better-value pick for everyday writing, research, and document work. Earlier releases such as Opus 4.8 and Fable 5 have been superseded and are no longer the current tier.
