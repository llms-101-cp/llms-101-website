Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of multimodal AI models, currently led by Gemini 3.7 Flash — the fast, low-cost workhorse released on August 13, 2026 — alongside the Gemini 3.1 Pro flagship and the lightweight Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) scores 56 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, and the model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window. With introductory pricing of $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens, it's well suited to coding, agent workflows, and analyzing long documents at scale.
