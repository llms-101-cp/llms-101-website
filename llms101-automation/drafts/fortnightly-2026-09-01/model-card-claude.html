Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models, spanning the fast, low-cost Haiku 4.5, the balanced Sonnet 5, the flagship Opus 5, and the most capable widely released model, Fable 5 (plus the restricted Mythos 5). Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026; it comes close to the frontier intelligence of Claude Fable 5 at half the price. Opus 5 offers a 1M-token context window, up to 128K output tokens, and list pricing of $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — the same rates as the previous-generation Opus 4.8. Anthropic positions Opus 5 as its everyday model for enterprises, knowledge workers, and developers , while for workloads that need the highest available capability, Claude Fable 5 is the recommended choice for long-horizon coding and agentic work.
