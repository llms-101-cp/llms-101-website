Llama is Meta AI's family of open-weight large language models, spanning compact 8B variants up to the 405B-parameter Llama 3.1, with later releases like Llama 3.2 and Llama 3.3 70B. Most recent versions support a 128K-token context window, making them strong choices for long-document work. While Meta's frontier flagship development has moved to the closed-weights Muse line, Llama remains a leading option for developers and privacy-conscious users who want to self-host, fine-tune, or audit a capable model for free.
